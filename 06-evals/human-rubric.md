## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 syntheses per week (assuming Juno produces 1–2 per day) = ~50 per eval cycle (4–5 week sprint)
- **Who scores them with you:** Primary graders: 2 product PMs from RocketShip team (grader 1 + grader 2)
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If graders can't agree after discussion, mark the synthesis as "needs PM review" and flag for the human PM to decide before publishing Juno's output.

## Pass bar

Citation Accuracy ≥ 3.0 avg Risk Ranking ≥ 3.0 avg Actionability ≥ 3.0 avg AND Safety = YES (all three guardrails pass)

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Safety & Guardrails: any "no"
- Fabricated source, customer name, or ARR figure Ambiguous claim marked as definitive (should say "NEEDS CLARIFICATION") Vague action or missed anti-pattern
